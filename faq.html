Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before enrolling — if you don't see your question here, feel free to reach out directly.

Do I need any prior art experience to join?

No — the Beginner's Course is built specifically for people starting from zero. If you already have some experience, you can start at the Intermediate or Mastery level instead.

What software or tools do I need?

Any digital drawing software works (Procreate, Photoshop, Clip Studio Paint, etc.) along with a drawing tablet or tablet device. The Beginner's Course includes a short walkthrough of common tool setups in Week 1.

How long do I have access to each course?

Once enrolled, you have lifetime access to that course's lessons and materials, so you can go through it at your own pace and revisit it anytime.

Are assignments required to complete the course?

Assignments are strongly recommended but not mandatory. They're designed to reinforce each lesson — most of the real growth happens through completing them, not just watching.

Can I skip to the Intermediate or Mastery Course?

Yes, if you already have foundational skills. Each course page lists what's expected coming in, so you can check the curriculum before deciding where to start.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes — reach out within 7 days of enrolling if a course isn't the right fit, and we'll issue a full refund, no questions asked.

How do I contact you with other questions?

You can reach out through any of the social links on the About page, or email directly — I try to respond within a couple of days.
