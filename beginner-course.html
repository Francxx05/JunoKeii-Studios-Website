Start From Zero

Start with the fundamentals that every artist needs. This course focuses on line, form, proportion, and light through concise lessons and practical exercises designed to strengthen your skills.

5
Sessions
10 hrs
Total Time
Beginner
Level

Each session builds on the skills from the previous one, starting with the basics of your tools and gradually introducing light and color. Here’s what you’ll learn each week.

Digital canvas & brush setup
Week 1

Introduction to Digital Art Tools

Start by getting familiar with your workspace, including canvas setup, brush settings, layers, and the essential tools you'll use throughout the course. You'll focus on what you actually need without getting overwhelmed by unnecessary features.

Gesture drawing studies
Week 2

Line & Shape Fundamentals

You’ll learn gesture drawing through quick, loose sketches that focus on movement, balance, and weight. By breaking figures down into simple shapes, you’ll build a stronger understanding of form and improve your drawing skills.

Anatomy & proportion reference
Week 3

Understanding Proportions

A first real pass at anatomy — head-to-body ratios, limb length, and how proportion changes with pose and perspective. You won't be expected to memorize everything at once; this week is about learning to look and measure.

Light source & shading study
Week 4

Intro to Shading & Light

Flat shapes start becoming believable forms once light enters the picture. You'll learn how to identify a light source, block in core shadow and highlight, and give your gesture and anatomy studies real dimension.

Color palette exploration
Week 5

Color Basics & Palette Building

To close out the course, you'll bring color into your work — building simple, cohesive palettes and understanding how color and value work together, setting you up for everything the Intermediate Course builds on.
