Develop Your Style

The final stage — moving beyond technique into a personal visual voice. Build a cohesive portfolio ready to show the world.

5
Sessions
14 hrs
Total Time
Advanced
Level

With fundamentals firmly in place, this course is about voice — turning technical skill into work that's recognizably, unmistakably yours, and finishing with a portfolio to show for it.

Style study & influence mapping
Week 1

Finding Your Visual Voice

Study the artistic choices that resonate with you and learn how to recognize the elements that make your work feel uniquely yours. Explore line, color, shapes, and visual language to develop a more intentional personal style.

Narrative composition sketches
Week 2

Storytelling Through Composition

Combine composition, character poses, expressions, and environments to communicate a clear story within a single image. Learn how to guide the viewer through a scene and create stronger visual narratives.

Mood lighting studies
Week 3

Advanced Lighting & Mood

Explore how lighting can shape emotion, atmosphere, and focus. Experiment with direction, intensity, temperature, and contrast to create scenes that feel calm, dramatic, mysterious, or unsettling.

Character design exploration
Week 4

Character Design Deep Dive

Develop original characters with strong silhouettes, memorable shapes, and clear personalities. Learn how to maintain their visual identity across different poses, expressions, and situations.

Final portfolio piece
Week 5

Building Your Portfolio

Bring everything together in a polished final piece that reflects your skills and artistic voice. Refine the anatomy, color, composition, lighting, and rendering to create artwork that is ready to showcase in your portfolio.
