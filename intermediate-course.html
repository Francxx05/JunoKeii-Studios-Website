Refine Your Craft

For artists who already know the basics and want to push further — deeper color theory, composition, and expressive linework.

5
Sessions
12 hrs
Total Time
Intermediate
Level

Assuming the fundamentals are in place, this course pushes into anatomy under motion, real color theory, and rendering — the skills that separate a sketch from a finished illustration.

Dynamic pose & gesture anatomy
Week 1

Dynamic Anatomy & Gesture

Push beyond static poses by studying how anatomy shifts with weight, balance, twisting, and movement. Learn to create figures that feel natural, energetic, and full of motion.

Color relationships & harmony
Week 2

Advanced Color Theory

Explore how color interacts through temperature, contrast, harmony, and value. Learn to build intentional palettes that strengthen the mood, atmosphere, and focus of your artwork.

Framing & focal point studies
Week 3

Composition & Framing

Learn how composition, framing, negative space, and focal points work together to control where the viewer looks. Develop stronger visual storytelling and more intentional image layouts.

Material & texture rendering
Week 4

Rendering Materials & Texture

Learn how light behaves across different surfaces such as skin, fabric, metal, and other materials. Practice using value, texture, and edges to make surfaces feel distinct and believable.

Environment & background sketching
Week 5

Environment & Background Basics

Learn to create simple environments that support and enhance your characters. Practice perspective, shapes, depth, and background details to give your illustrations a stronger sense of place
