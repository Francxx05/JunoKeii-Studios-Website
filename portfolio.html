Digital Artist & Illustrator

Hi, I'm Juno

I've been drawing for 11 years, specializing in digital illustration, character design, and D&D artwork. My work is recognized for its dramatic lighting and expressive characterization, with a focus on creating visually engaging characters with strong personalities. I have also been accepting commissions through VGen for the past 2 months, working with clients to bring their ideas to the canvas.

Outside of commissions, I enjoy developing original characters, exploring fantasy concepts, and studying visual references to continuously improve my art. My goal is to create artwork that combines strong design, storytelling, and expressive visual elements.
